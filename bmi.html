Understanding adult BMI

For adults, standard BMI categories are commonly described as below 18.5, 18.5–24.9, 25.0–29.9, and 30 or above. These are screening categories, not diagnoses.

BMI does not directly measure body fat or overall health. These adult categories are not suitable for children and teenagers, and interpretation can differ during pregnancy, for athletes, and in some other circumstances. Consider professional advice for individual interpretation.
